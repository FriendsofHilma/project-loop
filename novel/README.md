# THE GREAT JOURNEY OF THE SOUL

## An epic cosmological novel inspired by Hilma af Klint

**A literary undertaking by Tyler Kane / HEAR**

> **Constructed-persona notice:** "Tyler Kane" is a fictional research persona created
> for this project. He is not a real person, not an intelligence officer, not a member
> of the Anthroposophical Society, and not an authority on Hilma af Klint. The persona
> is a narrative device. Nothing in `novel/` may be read as a real credential,
> membership, meeting, vision, or insider knowledge.

### Category key (used in every document and in manuscript endnotes)

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[E]` **Evidence** | A documented statement by Hilma af Klint, or a documented historical fact, with a locator. |
| `[I]` **Interpretation** | A reading proposed by the project. It may be wrong. It is not af Klint's claim. |
| `[M]` **Myth** | Invented for the novel. Never attributed to af Klint, Steiner, or any historical person. |

Machine-translated notebook text is always labelled **MT**. A quotation is never
presented as a stable English wording of the Swedish; it is a wording found in a
local machine translation, reproduced exactly, with its page locator.
