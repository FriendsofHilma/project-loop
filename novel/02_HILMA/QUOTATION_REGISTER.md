# Quotation register

> **Constructed-persona notice:** "Tyler Kane" is a fictional research persona created
> for this project. He is not a real person, not an intelligence officer, not a member
> of the Anthroposophical Society, and not an authority on Hilma af Klint.

All locators are `FILENAME.pdf:PDF_PAGE` (page positions from 1). All notebook text is
**machine translation (MT)** from `hilma-translation/english-pdf/`, verified on the
whole page with `python novel/tools/page.py FILENAME.pdf:PAGE` on 2026-11-22.
Translation oddities are preserved exactly. Dates marked *internal* are dates written
inside the page, not necessarily the date of the whole notebook entry.

Grades: **A** exact and clear; **B** exact but translation/date unstable;
**C** paraphrase only. Movements: **I** Inspiration, **R** Revelation, **M** Remembrance.
