## 2. Three criteria [E]

From S-02:

- **Utvecklande:** Methods, working methods or operations developed in the project must benefit the target group. It must be motivated why a development project is needed to test whether the method/working method works. It must differ from ordinary operations.
- **Delaktighet:** The target group shall have influence and participate actively according to their own conditions in planning, implementation and continued activity. Arvsfonden assesses participation during application writing, during the project and after the project.
- **Överlevnad:** There must be a feasible, realistic plan for how experiences and results continue to benefit the target group after the project period. The plan must state what lives on, where, who is responsible and how it is financed.

All three criteria must be met. [E]

## 3. Target groups [E]

From S-01, the four selectable groups are:

- Barn 0–11 år
- Ungdomar 12–25 år
- Personer som har fyllt 26 år och har funktionsnedsättning
- Äldre personer som har fyllt 65 år

The project must benefit at least one of them. The application asks for one primary group and may also mark others. [E]

## 5. Organisation, attachments and signing [E][?]

From S-01, S-04, S-05 and S-06:

- The applying organisation must have conducted activity in Sweden for at least one year. [E]
- The project must differ from ordinary operations. [E]
- At least one result goal and one effect goal are required. [E]
- Attachments depend on organisation form; a budget in Arvsfonden's template is required for all forms. [E]
- The application is signed by behörig företrädare (firmatecknare) using e-legitimation. [E]
- The static question page still states: "Ja, vi har läst och förstått de generella villkoren för projektstöd (version 6.0 A) ..." [E]
- The conditions page states that version **7.0 A** applies to project/effect support decided from **2027-01-01**, while **6.0 A** applies to decisions during 2023-04-01 – 2026-12-31. [E]
- **Unresolved [?]:** which version a future application will be assessed under. The draft must not silently select one.

Budget rules relevant to this draft [E]:

- Only costs financed by the requested Arvsfonden funds are included in the project budget.
- Costs must be based on qualified estimates such as quotations or reality-based information.
- General overhead or unspecified costs are not granted; costs must be direct and under the correct heading.
- Budget may not include salary costs for writing the application.
- Fees to reference groups, steering groups and expert groups are at most SEK 500 per person and occasion.
- Evaluation costs and recruitment costs may not be in the budget.
- Collaboration partners' costs go under "Externa tjänster"; no advance invoicing.
