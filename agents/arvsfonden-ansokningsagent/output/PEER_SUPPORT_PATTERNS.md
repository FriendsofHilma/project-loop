## Project-by-project extraction [E]

### Peer Support — Svenska Röda Korset

Official description, abbreviated and paraphrased:

- Aim: more people should receive support and motivation to complete trauma treatment, feel agency and hope for the future.
- Target group: people with migration background experiencing mental ill-health due to torture, war or flight experiences.
- Method: test and develop Peer Support **from the target group's experience and conditions**.
- Former patients are trained as Peer Supporters; together with patients and caregivers they form structures and activities.
- Intended change: more people seek care, are motivated to start treatment and complete trauma treatment to a greater extent.
- Geography: Göteborg, Skövde and Vänersborg.
- Survival: after the project, experiences and results are to be evaluated, spread to more actors meeting the target group and implemented at all Red Cross treatment centres.

### Peer support med STIL — Stiftelsen Independent Living Utveckling

- Aim: develop a user-friendly **digital toolkit for self-organising peer support**.
- Target group: persons with disabilities, both individuals and groups.
- Principle: find knowledge, tips, examples and concrete methods for organising peer support **from one's own conditions and needs**, and **independent of professional operations and outside experts**.
- Also documents how identity can be harmed when life conditions and participation are repeatedly questioned, e.g. in assessments linked to personal assistance.
- Peer support is described as offering tools that question internalised norms and support freedom from created limitations.
- Material is kept freely available to everyone.
- STIL and project partners are responsible for dissemination, continued stewardship and further development.

### Mitt Liv, mitt val! — Stiftelsen Institutet för Independent Living

- Aim: strengthen the right to self-determination and equality before the law for young adults, adults and older people who, due to disability, need decision support from a guardian/administrator to make well-considered decisions.
- Methods named: study circles, peer-support and so-called decision-support groups with practical support in decision-making.
- The project compares and tests decision-support models from, among others, Norway and Canada.
- Survival: experiences and lessons are to be used in the foundation's continued process work and by partners; tested methods/models are intended to influence current practice in the longer term. The official page text ends mid-sentence after "påverka nuvarande".

### Pappor i separation — Män för jämställdhet

- Aim: create methods and material to support children and fathers in a separation process; jämställt föräldraskap with the child's best interest in focus.
- Target group: children with experience of separated parents and fathers considering, undergoing or having undergone separation.
- Method: combine several existing methods and tools developed by different collaboration partners; conversation methods, mentorship (peer-support) and relationship-strengthening activities for fathers and children.
- Material is intended to work both for the individual and for groups.
- Survival plan: spread methods and material via members, local groups and associations, and package training based on project experiences.
- Reported after-project results [E]: platform pappor.se; training for fathers and group leaders; routines for identifying fathers needing extra support and for handling violence in the family; about 6 000 professionals reached; about 300 fathers reached through conflict-preventing and parent-supporting efforts; 120 fathers in conversation groups; about 200 questions and answers in the "Pappa till pappa" forum; over 200 children reached through workshops and films; 15 000 visitors to pappor.se and 216 000 film views.
- Continuation [E]: pappor.se remains; the organisation later received verksamhetsstöd from Folkhälsomyndigheten to further develop pappor.se and father groups, with focus on mental health.

## Cross-project patterns [I]

| Pattern | Seen in | Relevance for "ett arbetssätt" |
|---|---|---|
| Start from the target group's lived experience, not from a finished service | All four, explicitly in Peer Support and STIL | Makes the working method testable and legitimate under Delaktighet |
| Train peers or affected persons to carry roles | Peer Support, Pappor i separation, Mitt Liv, mitt val! | Converts participation into an operating method rather than a consultation exercise |
| Combine and adapt existing methods instead of claiming invention | Pappor i separation, Mitt Liv, mitt val! | Supports Utvecklande when adaptation to a new group/context is explicit |
| Produce open, reusable material | STIL, Pappor i separation | Supports Överlevnad beyond the funded period |
| Name a responsible steward after the project | STIL, Pappor i separation, Mitt Liv, mitt val! | Answers "who owns and maintains it?" |
| Test in several concrete settings | Peer Support (three cities), Mitt Liv, mitt val! (models from Norway/Canada) | Gives a development project a reason to exist |
| Link dissemination to existing networks rather than a new single event | All four | Avoids the "single arrangement" rejection ground |
